## 4.161 DBA\_AUDIT\_POLICY\_COLUMNS

`DBA_AUDIT_POLICY_COLUMNS` describes all fine-grained auditing policy columns in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_AUDIT_POLICY_COLUMNS`.
