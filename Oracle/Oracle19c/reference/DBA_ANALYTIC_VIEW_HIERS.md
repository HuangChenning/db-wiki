## 4.74 DBA\_ANALYTIC\_VIEW\_HIERS

`DBA_ANALYTIC_VIEW_HIERS` describes the hierarchies in all analytic views in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_ANALYTIC_VIEW_HIERS`.
