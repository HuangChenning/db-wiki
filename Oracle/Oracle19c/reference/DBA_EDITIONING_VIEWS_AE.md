## 4.275 DBA\_EDITIONING\_VIEWS\_AE

`DBA_EDITIONING_VIEWS_AE` describes all editioning views (across all editions) in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_EDITIONING_VIEWS_AE`.
