## 4.138 DBA\_ATTRIBUTE\_DIM\_LEVEL\_ATTRS

`DBA_ATTRIBUTE_DIM_LEVEL_ATTRS` describes the attributes of the levels of all attribute dimensions in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_ATTRIBUTE_DIM_LEVEL_ATTRS`.
