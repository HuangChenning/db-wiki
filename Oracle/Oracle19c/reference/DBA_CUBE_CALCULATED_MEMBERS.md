## 4.229 DBA\_CUBE\_CALCULATED\_MEMBERS

`DBA_CUBE_CALCULATED_MEMBERS` describes the calculated members for all OLAP cube dimensions in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_CUBE_CALCULATED_MEMBERS`.
