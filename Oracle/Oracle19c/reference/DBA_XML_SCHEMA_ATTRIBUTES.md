## 6.135 DBA\_XML\_SCHEMA\_ATTRIBUTES

`DBA_XML_SCHEMA_ATTRIBUTES` describes all the attributes and their properties. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_XML_SCHEMA_ATTRIBUTES`.
