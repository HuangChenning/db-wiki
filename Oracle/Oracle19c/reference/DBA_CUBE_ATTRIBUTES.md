## 4.227 DBA\_CUBE\_ATTRIBUTES

`DBA_CUBE_ATTRIBUTES` describes the attributes for all OLAP cube dimensions in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_CUBE_ATTRIBUTES`.
