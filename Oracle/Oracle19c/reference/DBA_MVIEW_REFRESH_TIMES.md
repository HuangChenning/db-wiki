## 5.196 DBA\_MVIEW\_REFRESH\_TIMES

`DBA_MVIEW_REFRESH_TIMES` describes refresh times of all materialized views in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_MVIEW_REFRESH_TIMES`.
