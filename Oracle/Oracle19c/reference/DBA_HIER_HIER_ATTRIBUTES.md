## 4.335 DBA\_HIER\_HIER\_ATTRIBUTES

`DBA_HIER_HIER_ATTRIBUTES` describes the hierarchical attributes of all hierarchies in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_HIER_HIER_ATTRIBUTES`.
