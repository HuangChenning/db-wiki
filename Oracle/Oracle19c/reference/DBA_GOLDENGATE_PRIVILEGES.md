## 4.321 DBA\_GOLDENGATE\_PRIVILEGES

`DBA_GOLDENGATE_PRIVILEGES` displays details about Oracle GoldenGate privileges. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_GOLDENGATE_PRIVILEGES`.
