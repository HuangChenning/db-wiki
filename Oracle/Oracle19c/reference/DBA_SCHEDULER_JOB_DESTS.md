## 5.328 DBA\_SCHEDULER\_JOB\_DESTS

`DBA_SCHEDULER_JOB_DESTS` displays information about the state of all jobs in the database at each of their destinations. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_SCHEDULER_JOB_DESTS`.
