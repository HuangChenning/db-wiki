## 6.166 DBA\_XTERNAL\_TAB\_PARTITIONS

`DBA_XTERNAL_TAB_PARTITIONS` describes partition-level information for partitioned external tables in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_XTERNAL_TAB_PARTITIONS`.
