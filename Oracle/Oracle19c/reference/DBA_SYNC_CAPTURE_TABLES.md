## 6.27 DBA\_SYNC\_CAPTURE\_TABLES

`DBA_SYNC_CAPTURE_TABLES` displays information about all tables in the database that are captured by synchronous captures. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_SYNC_CAPTURE_TABLES`.
