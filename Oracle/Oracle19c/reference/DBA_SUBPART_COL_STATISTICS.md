## 6.19 DBA\_SUBPART\_COL\_STATISTICS

`DBA_SUBPART_COL_STATISTICS` provides column statistics and histogram information for all subpartitions in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_SUBPART_COL_STATISTICS`.
