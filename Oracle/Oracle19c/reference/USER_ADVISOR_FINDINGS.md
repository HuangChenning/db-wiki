## 6.273 USER\_ADVISOR\_FINDINGS

`USER_ADVISOR_FINDINGS` displays the findings discovered by the advisors owned by the current user. Its columns (except for `OWNER`) are the same as those in `DBA_ADVISOR_FINDINGS`.
