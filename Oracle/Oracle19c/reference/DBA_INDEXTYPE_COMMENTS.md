## 5.116 DBA\_INDEXTYPE\_COMMENTS

`DBA_INDEXTYPE_COMMENTS` displays comments for all user-defined indextypes in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_INDEXTYPE_COMMENTS`.
