## 4.234 DBA\_CUBE\_DIMENSIONALITY

`DBA_CUBE_DIMENSIONALITY` describes the dimension order for all OLAP cubes in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_CUBE_DIMENSIONALITY`.
