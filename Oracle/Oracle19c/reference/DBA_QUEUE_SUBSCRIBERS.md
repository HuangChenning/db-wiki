## 5.258 DBA\_QUEUE\_SUBSCRIBERS

`DBA_QUEUE_SUBSCRIBERS` displays all subscribers on all queues in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_QUEUE_SUBSCRIBERS`.
