## 6.606 USER\_POLICY\_GROUPS

`USER_POLICY_GROUPS` describes the policy groups defined for the synonyms, tables, and views owned by the current user. Its columns (except for `OBJECT_OWNER`) are the same as those in `ALL_POLICY_GROUPS`.
