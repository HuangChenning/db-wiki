## 6.435 USER\_DIM\_CHILD\_OF

`USER_DIM_CHILD_OF` describes a hierarchical relationship of 1 to n between pairs of levels in dimensions owned by the current user. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_DIM_CHILD_OF`.
