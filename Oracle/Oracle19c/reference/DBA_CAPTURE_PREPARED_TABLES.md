## 4.189 DBA\_CAPTURE\_PREPARED\_TABLES

`DBA_CAPTURE_PREPARED_TABLES` displays information about all tables prepared for instantiation at the local database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_CAPTURE_PREPARED_TABLES`.
