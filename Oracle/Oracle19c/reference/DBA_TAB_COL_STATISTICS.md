## 6.30 DBA\_TAB\_COL\_STATISTICS

`DBA_TAB_COL_STATISTICS` contains column statistics and histogram information extracted from `DBA_TAB_COLUMNS`. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_TAB_COL_STATISTICS`.
