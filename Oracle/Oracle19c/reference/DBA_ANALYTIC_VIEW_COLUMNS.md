## 4.63 DBA\_ANALYTIC\_VIEW\_COLUMNS

`DBA_ANALYTIC_VIEW_COLUMNS` describes the columns of all analytic views in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_ANALYTIC_VIEW_COLUMNS`.
