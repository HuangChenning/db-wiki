## 6.301 USER\_ANALYTIC\_VIEW\_CALC\_MEAS

`USER_ANALYTIC_VIEW_CALC_MEAS` describes the calculated measures in the analytic views owned by the current user. Its columns (except for `OWNER`) are the same as those in `ALL_ANALYTIC_VIEW_CALC_MEAS`.
