## 4.292 DBA\_EXTERNAL\_LOCATIONS

`DBA_EXTERNAL_LOCATIONS` describes the locations (data sources) of all external tables in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_EXTERNAL_LOCATIONS`.
