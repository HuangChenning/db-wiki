## 6.328 USER\_ANALYTIC\_VIEW\_MEAS\_CLASS

`USER_ANALYTIC_VIEW_MEAS_CLASS` describes the classifications of the measures of the analytic views owned by the current user. Its columns (except for `OWNER`) are the same as those in `ALL_ANALYTIC_VIEW_MEAS_CLASS`.
