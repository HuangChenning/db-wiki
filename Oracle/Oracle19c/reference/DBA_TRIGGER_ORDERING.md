## 6.55 DBA\_TRIGGER\_ORDERING

`DBA_TRIGGER_ORDERING` describes all triggers in the database that have `FOLLOWS` or `PRECEDES` ordering. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_TRIGGER_ORDERING`.
