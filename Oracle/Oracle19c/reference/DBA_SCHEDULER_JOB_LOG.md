## 5.329 DBA\_SCHEDULER\_JOB\_LOG

`DBA_SCHEDULER_JOB_LOG` displays log information for all Scheduler jobs in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_SCHEDULER_JOB_LOG`.
