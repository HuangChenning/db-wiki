## 4.85 DBA\_ANALYTIC\_VIEW\_MEAS\_CLASS

`DBA_ANALYTIC_VIEW_MEAS_CLASS` describes the classifications of the measures of all analytic views in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_ANALYTIC_VIEW_MEAS_CLASS`.
