## 4.243 DBA\_CUBE\_VIEW\_COLUMNS

`DBA_CUBE_VIEW_COLUMNS` describes the columns of relational views of all OLAP cubes in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_CUBE_VIEW_COLUMNS`.
