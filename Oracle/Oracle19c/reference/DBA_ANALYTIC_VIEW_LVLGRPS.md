## 4.83 DBA\_ANALYTIC\_VIEW\_LVLGRPS

`DBA_ANALYTIC_VIEW_LVLGRPS` describes the analytic view measure and level groups of all analytic views in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_ANALYTIC_VIEW_LVLGRPS`.
