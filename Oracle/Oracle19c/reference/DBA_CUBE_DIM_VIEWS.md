## 4.233 DBA\_CUBE\_DIM\_VIEWS

`DBA_CUBE_DIM_VIEWS` describes the relational views of all OLAP dimensions in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_CUBE_DIM_VIEWS`.
