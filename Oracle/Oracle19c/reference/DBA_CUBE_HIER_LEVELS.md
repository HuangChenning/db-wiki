## 4.236 DBA\_CUBE\_HIER\_LEVELS

`DBA_CUBE_HIER_LEVELS` describes the hierarchy levels for all OLAP cube dimensions in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_CUBE_HIER_LEVELS`.
