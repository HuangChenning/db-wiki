## 4.58 DBA\_ANALYTIC\_VIEW\_CALC\_MEAS

`DBA_ANALYTIC_VIEW_CALC_MEAS` describes the calculated measures in all analytic views in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_ANALYTIC_VIEW_CALC_MEAS`.
