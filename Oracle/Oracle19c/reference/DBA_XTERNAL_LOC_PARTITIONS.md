## 6.163 DBA\_XTERNAL\_LOC\_PARTITIONS

`DBA_XTERNAL_LOC_PARTITIONS` describes partition-level locations in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_XTERNAL_LOC_PARTITIONS`.
