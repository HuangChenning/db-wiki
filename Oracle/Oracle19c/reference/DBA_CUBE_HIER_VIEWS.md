## 4.238 DBA\_CUBE\_HIER\_VIEWS

`DBA_CUBE_HIER_VIEWS` describes the hierarchies for all OLAP cube dimensions in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_CUBE_HIER_VIEWS`.
